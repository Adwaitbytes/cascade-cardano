Executive summary

The Singapore coffee shop market was valued at SGD 1.1 billion in 2022. Specialty coffee consumption is rising, particularly among younger demographics interested in unique experiences and ethically sourced beans. Online sales and delivery apps are key distribution channels. The average revenue per user is projected at SGD 130.70 in 2024. A subscription brand can enter by focusing on direct-to-consumer online sales and partnerships with delivery platforms, targeting urban professionals and coffee enthusiasts.
