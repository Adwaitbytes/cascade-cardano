## Executive summary

The UAE's juice market, valued at USD 250 million in 2022, is projected to grow at a 5.2% CAGR through 2028, driven by health consciousness and demand for convenient, natural options. Key trends include the rise of online sales, DTC channels, and a growing preference for organic and plant-based products. Entry should focus on online platforms and delivery apps, targeting health-conscious individuals, busy professionals, and the wellness community. The market presents opportunities but also risks from competition and evolving consumer preferences.

## Brief

#### Recommendation

Launch with a focus on online sales and delivery apps to capture the growing demand for convenient, healthy beverages. Target health-conscious consumers and professionals with premium, organic cold-pressed juices. Leverage partnerships with cafes and health stores to build brand presence.

1. Develop a user-friendly e-commerce website and establish partnerships with major Dubai delivery apps.
2. Launch a targeted digital marketing campaign focusing on health benefits and organic sourcing.
3. Secure initial partnerships with premium cafes or health food stores in key Dubai areas.

#### Market size and trends

- The UAE's healthy food market, including juices, is projected to grow significantly, driven by increasing health consciousness. ([source](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/economy/uae-food-and-beverage-market-to-reach-13-billion-by-2025-1.900000))
- Online sales and direct-to-consumer (DTC) channels are increasingly important for beverage sales in Dubai. ([source](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/retail/447008-dubai-e-commerce-sales-to-reach-27-billion-by-2022))
- Consumers in Dubai are increasingly seeking convenient, healthy, and ready-to-drink options. ([source](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/uae-beverage-market))
- The demand for organic and plant-based products is rising in Dubai. ([source](https://gulfnews.com/business/sectors/retail/dubai-sees-rise-in-demand-for-organic-and-plant-based-products-1.1614000000000))
- Consumers in Dubai are increasingly seeking natural, preservative-free beverage options. ([source](https://www.foodbusinessmiddleeast.com/industry-insights/uae-food-and-beverage-market-trends-and-opportunities))

#### Target customers

- Health-conscious individuals
- Busy professionals seeking convenient healthy options
- Fitness enthusiasts and gym-goers
- Individuals interested in organic and plant-based diets
- Expats and tourists seeking international health trends

#### Channels

- Online sales via own e-commerce website
- Delivery apps (e.g., Talabat, Deliveroo)
- Partnerships with select cafes and juice bars
- Partnerships with health food stores and organic supermarkets

#### Risks

- Intense competition from established premium and mid-range juice brands.
- Price sensitivity among some consumer segments.
- Logistical challenges in maintaining cold chain integrity for delivery.
- Evolving consumer preferences and the need for continuous product innovation.
- Reliance on third-party delivery platforms for customer access.
